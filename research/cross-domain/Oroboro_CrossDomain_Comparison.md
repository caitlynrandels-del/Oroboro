### 3. Which runs were conjunctive, and which were not

An integrated loop, as the systems are defined, is not a checklist of components exercised in separate blocks. It is one cycle in which each stage feeds the next: Genesis origin → reduced parts → Oasis salience → PrinciplesGate → NaCl seat / mesh consensus → Dictator stream → Transcriber → Levy assess → Nexus register → damped delta reconciliation → homeostasis / next Oasis threshold → niche repair → consensus refresh → back to Genesis closure.

**Published native-mesh restart — partially conjunctive, with ledger gaps.** The monthly state really was the polarity mesh. Oasis admission, lock/frustrated seating, heat-driven repair, homeostasis rest-load, and a damper term on occupancy were inside the step and did feed the next month. Dictator and Transcriber, however, chronicled only when recording was on, after the fact, and did not feed Levy or the damper. Levy totals were a consistency check at recombine, not an in-step input. PrinciplesGate and the sponge were absent. Because occupancy stayed below the homeostasis rest load of 40, the occupancy-only damper never engaged. That is why the published note can say, correctly for that wiring, that damped reconciliation was implemented and silent.

**Launch Goldilocks case study — components exercised in isolated blocks, then packaged.** `run_magnetic_launch_case_study.py` calls `run_oasis()`, `run_meshes()`, `goldilocks_sweep()`, `damper_study()`, and `run_relational()` as separate stages. The raw log itself labels “Oasis isolated fresh world.” Oasis scored the engineering-arc text (commit at exactly 0.65 with a lab coordinate; AdaptOrDie blocked a missing coordinate) but did not choose rail configurations. Mesh locks did not change flights. Relational recombine used damper transmissibility after the fact. The `Math.random` stub was correctly avoided.

**Later launch addenda — mostly not conjunctive.**

- `run_ondulatory_addendum.py`: NaCl polarity and a Laplacian on the placeholder tree; no Oasis, Nexus loop, Dictator, or homeostasis.
- `run_dreamcatcher_weave.py`: NaCl polarity plus an accounting-length recombine (`matchedWholeSystem: true` at 7.28×10⁻¹²); not the full loop.
- `run_goal_seek_recycle.py`: an Oasis-*style* search utility (commit 0.65 / buffer 0.325) and a compact onboard loop (three noisy votes, median as mesh consensus, Levy on |error| and q, Oasis commit of a γ correction). That onboard loop *is* conjunctive among those four pieces, and it is the loop that **hurt** insertion. Genesis, Dictator, PrinciplesGate, and the NaCl polarity mesh were not in the flight integrator.
- `run_tilt_torque.py` and `run_geography_addendum.py`: **no Oroboro component calls**. Geography is a physics re-evaluation of recycled exits.

### 4. Compare and contrast

Both domains, read through the same loop, say that leftovers are set at admission and release, and that downstream correction does not empty them. In the published native mesh, only an upstream Oasis threshold (0.80–0.85) or a cut in organ permissiveness shrinks both colonies and the dormant pool; post-settling maintenance and colonization barriers clear colonies and leave ~10.5 cells, mostly on lockable hoop edges, not on odd-loop edges. Frustrated-edge count moves opposite the pool (Spearman −0.819) because adaptive repair — the same heat-window mechanism used as feather regrowth on the dart — unlocks odd faces in the busy settings that also fill the pool. On the launch side, the 407–431 m/s no-stage leftovers are set at the evac-tube mouth (pusher hold, 80 km, a few degrees of tilt). Equatorial rotation cannot be subtracted from that gap; it overshoots and leaves 375–398 m/s of ellipse shaping. The 256 m/s staged figure was an incomplete-coast artifact (true 1,148 m/s). A compact onboard loop that really did wire votes, consensus, Levy, and Oasis made the unstaged apogee fall from 115 km to 50 km and the staged residual rise from 325 to 744 m/s. Feathers and atmospheric tiers add lower-tier glide, not orbital Δv. Heat-driven weave repair sits in a narrow mid-speed band and ablates the filament budget on the 17,000 mph evac path.

The named parameter that diverges is tilt, and the loops themselves are not the reservoir. A 5° spin-plane tilt at an 80 km mouth is the in-set way to set climb angle and satisfy Y(a); a 0° release is closer in vacuum residual (333 m/s) but does not climb, and a steeper tilt inflates the ellipse. The same named bias on the cascade’s circulation ring concentrates organ encounters and, in the published 80-seed probe, raises colony probability from 0.165 to 0.348 (tilt 0.3) and 0.438 (tilt 0.6), with the pool rising from 10.40 to 13.22 and 15.19. Loops tax colonization (published baseline 18.0% versus 20.6% on a tree ablation) but do not set pool size — consistent with the weave follow-on, where odd triangular cells leave 59 of 175 edges open, and that frustration is a lock-set property, not a stored mass. Geography plays the same role as organ permissiveness: site latitude is an admission condition on the 0° leftover (Baikonur 66 m/s; LAS / Mount Charleston 118–119 m/s), while the usual coastal and equatorial list is a downrange-safety and inclination-floor constraint, not a 1:1 Δv gift. Shared machinery, when it is actually wired, is Genesis as origin, Nexus as register, Levy-weighted discrete pulses, Oasis 0.65 / 0.325 routing, invert-and-restabilize polarity, and state reduction that is supposed to recombine to a matched whole. The missing conjunctive piece in the published cascade was that Levy and Dictator did not feed the damper; the missing piece in geography is that the loop was not run at all.

### 5. Conjunctive-use check (recycled, labeled new)

A 40-setting subsample of the 454, 16 seeds × five cancers, was used only to ask whether the frustrated-versus-pool sign survives; it is not a replacement of the published 454-setting correlation.

Isolated 200-run baseline values sit close to the published tables (dormant **10.480** versus 10.355–10.406; colony probability **0.175** versus 0.178–0.180). The occupancy-only damper remains at pressure **0** in every isolated setting.

AdaptOrDie re-seat almost never fires (adapted mean 0.00–0.01), so the change is not a mid-course reseating law. It is the loop tightening **upstream admission**.

Charges were a **labeled interpretation** of already-documented association signs (complementary / negative association = opposite charge, can lock; same-direction or unreduced leftover = same charge, stays open). Damping meters spread; it does not make a mixed leftover look like a closed system. That is the same damper lesson as the Goldilocks pulse-train recombine, now applied across domains.

PrinciplesGate AdaptOrDie blocked only the missing-coordinate case, as designed. In sequence, the first fact committed and later items buffered because novelty fell with word overlap — facts and false closures alike. This is the Goldilocks caveat again: the gate scores and routes; it is not a scientific verdict. It is reported so that this pass is not mistaken for confirmation.
